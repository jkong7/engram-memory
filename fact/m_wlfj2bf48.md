# Claude launch shortcuts (skhd)

Claude launchers live in ~/.local/bin/ (worker lib/claude-open-tiled.py), open Terminal.app sessions via osascript, and are bound in ~/.config/skhd/skhdrc. ⌘1 starts a fresh ~/dev session. ⌘2 resumes the most recent ~/dev session not already open, detected by Terminal window title only. ⌘3 reuses open windows. ⌘4 runs the job-search apply run.
