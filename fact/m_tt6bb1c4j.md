# Chartside excludes licensed coding data from the repo

Chartside's repo does not contain CPT descriptions, NCCI/MUE bundling and unit edits, or coverage data, because they fall under AMA and Medicare license terms. Official ICD-10-CM, HCPCS, fee schedule and HCC V28 files are bundled, and licensed sets are loaded by the operator after accepting those terms.
