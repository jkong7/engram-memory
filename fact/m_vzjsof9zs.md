# Caffeinate toggle setup

Jonny's caffeinate toggle is a Raycast script at ~/raycast-scripts/caffeinate-toggle.sh. It runs caffeinate -d, tracks its PID in ~/.caffeinate-toggle.pid, and flashes /Applications/CaffeineHUD.app showing ON or OFF.
