# My Notes Raycast extension

My Notes is a Raycast extension built 2026-10-04 at ~/dev/raycast-notes. It reads and edits the Markdown notes in the Notes vault, grouped by folder. Enter reads, ⌘E edits, ⌘N creates, ⌘⇧S captures a screenshot into a note, and ⌘⇧V pastes a clipboard image.
