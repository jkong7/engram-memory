# Abridge CDI overarching goal

Jonathan's Abridge CDI team aimed to take as much billing and coding work as possible off manual CDI staff and hospital coders. By the time a clinician clicks send-to-EHR, diagnoses should already have specificity, ICD-10 and HCC attachments, and be billable.
