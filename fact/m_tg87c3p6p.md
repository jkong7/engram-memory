Agreed 2026-10-02 after deep research (~/dev/reports/Personal Jarvis stack on Mac.md, notes in ~/dev/research_notes/Personal Jarvis stack on Mac/).

Layers: Todoist = what to do ([[todoist-setup]]); ~/brain vault (markdown, Obsidian, own git) = what he knows (me/, people/, projects/, ideas/, readings/, classes/, decisions/, daily/ handoffs, inbox/); repos in ~/dev + ~/dev/reports = what he makes; Claude memory files + episodic-memory = what Claude knows; connectors + Chrome = reach; cloud routines = background; flow-kit hooks = guards ([[flow-kit-repo]]).
