# Chicago Winter/Spring 2027 internship/co-op outreach push and Jonathan's own email template to use for it

Jonathan will likely finish his bachelor's in Fall quarter 2026 but stay in Evanston for Winter and Spring 2027 (Jan to Jun) and wants a Chicago internship/co-op then. Outreach rows live in the ledger as `<company>-chicago-winter-spring-outreach`.

Rules he set (2026-09-24): Chicago-based companies only; never say he is graduating; frame as a BS/MS student eager for a winter and/or spring internship or co-op; attach the Internship resume (2028); recruiters/people team over CEOs; if there is an application front door, apply AND email; no repeat companies. Postings that explicitly mention intern, new grad or winter/spring come first.

**Why:** He rewrote the drafts himself before sending, so his template is the canonical voice for this push.
**How to apply:** Draft new Chicago outreach from this template. Swap the company name and the company-specific sentences (what caught his attention, the closing mission line). Keep the rest verbatim, including his tone. Related: [[frq-answer-bank]], [[linkedin-premium-outreach]].

Template (sent to FourKites, 2026-09-24):

Hi FourKites team,

I’m Jonny, a Northwestern computer science BS/MS student studying Computer Science. I would love to get involved with what FourKites is building through an engineering internship or co-op (just any opportunity to get involved!) in Chicago this winter and/or spring quarter (Approx Jan to Jun). FourKites’ work connecting live shipment, inventory, and order data caught my attention because knowing something is delayed is useful, but helping a team decide what to do next is even more valuable. From what I've seen, customers love the products and I'm super on board with the mission!

A little bit about myself: this summer, I worked on Abridge’s Clinical Documentation Integrity team and shipped three features to production for its 30,000+ clinician partners: a Level of Service layer for billing, a Query Engine that asks clinicians to clarify diagnoses, and improvements to diagnosis search. My experiences in engineering and software have given me a simple motto and that's to make things correct. Working with AI coding tools taught me that generating code faster only helps if you can verify what it does. I care about clear architecture, repeatable checks, and reviewing the outcomes that matter to users. I’m extremely passionate about this and about pushing the frontier of AI and software in 2026’s chaotic environment, especially getting these tools into real processes that solve real problems. FourKites’ work turning real-time supply chain data into actions that operations teams can trust is the sort of practical work I’d like to help build.

There’s a lot of AI/software being pumped out nowadays so I want to be on board with something that tackles an existing human problem and doesn’t just create more noise!

Would you be open to a quick chat or a brief back-and-forth here (I realize you're probably very busy :) )? My resume is attached.

Thanks,
Jonny
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jonathankong1/)

Must read human and flowing, never long or rambly (sounds AI-generated). Example shape: "...a real signal in how drivers actually find parking, and there's so much value and impact in getting that right."
