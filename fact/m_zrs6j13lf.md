# Multi-user worlds and accounts

Burner supports several players, each with a separate world. Guests are identified by a cookie. Since 2026-10-10 players can sign in with an email link through Supabase Auth, which moves a guest's life onto the account. When multi-user is on, daily AI-call and photo caps are per user, and city travel is isolated per player.
