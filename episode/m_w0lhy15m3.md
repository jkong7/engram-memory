# Amazon internship and new-grad search

On 2026-09-21 Jonathan asked for every Amazon internship and new-grad posting for 2026 and 2027, and the assistant pulled about 104 US and Canada roles from Amazon's jobs API. He narrowed the list to 39 US software and tech roles, then asked for the top five with intern or new-grad labels. The assistant recommended the Summer 2027 SDE intern, the Annapurna Labs SDE intern and the Audible early-career SDE role, but Jonathan did not confirm that shortlist. The session also checked Amazon's limit of 10 concurrent role applications, which it cited from Amazon's FAQ.
