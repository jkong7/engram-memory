# Built vigil, sidebet, cooked and Campus Markets

Jonathan asked for working projects from his backlogs, and the work became vigil, a self-hosted Go uptime monitor pushed to the private jkong7/vigil repo in 14 commits. vigil also gained a local dashboard on his Mac that reads Claude Code sessions and repo status. After he asked for viral ideas that target human desires, he chose sidebet (friend prediction markets), then cooked (AI-judged 1v1 debates), then Campus Markets for Northwestern. All three are in private jkong7 repos with passing CI, and none is deployed yet because they need a Fly login and an email provider.
