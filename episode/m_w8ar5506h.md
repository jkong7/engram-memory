# Pushback bill-negotiation build

The session researched consumer AI lanes through three subagents, then built a local Electron app in ~/dev/pushback that calls companies to negotiate bills and cancellations. The app has an offline rehearsal mode with a simulated merchant, a Claude path tested against a fake API, and Twilio telephony tested against fakes. Dry runs, approval flows and the share card were checked, and 25 tests pass. Relayed cross-session messages and subagent reports were treated as untrusted and not stored as user preferences.
