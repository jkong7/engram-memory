# Fresh-company application wave

Jonny asked for software internship and new-grad roles at companies not already in the ledger, and the agent found 63 open Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever postings at new companies. Applications were filled in Chrome in batches of 5, 10 and 10 with Submit left to Jonny. Early fills had dropdowns that displayed values without saving them, so the agent re-entered them and verified each field against the form's saved state. Ones that were repeats or ineligible, such as ONE Finance (OnePay repeat) and Giftogram (graduation requirement), were dropped. Jonny marked 28 companies as applied on 2026-10-01 and they were blocklisted.
