# Job boards startup research and ranked artifact

Jonny asked for deep research on startups on his job boards that he had not yet contacted, ranking them by growth, funding, revenue, virality and hiring. The assistant excluded 1,195 companies already in his ledger or queue, ran six research batches over 149 startups, and dropped acquired companies. It spot-checked the biggest claims against sources and ranked the results into tiers. Jonny then asked for it as a Claude artifact, which was published with a Tier 1 list, skip list and searchable table.
