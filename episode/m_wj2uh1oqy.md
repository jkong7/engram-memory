# honey.ai Candy AI clone

Jonny asked for a front-end clone of candy.ai and the assistant built honey.ai in ~/dev/honey, running at localhost:3210. It includes a home grid, chat backed by Claude streaming (demo replies until ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is set), a 13-step character builder, and premium/token mocks. Later the character portraits and chat photo requests were switched to Gemini 2.5 Flash Image on Vertex AI in project persona-onboarding-jk, producing 24 portraits for about $1 total.
