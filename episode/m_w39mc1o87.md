# Parallel job application batches

Jonny asked the agent to fill job applications in parallel with another session, stopping before Submit and logging each form in the Application Ledger. This session filled 62 forms across several batches, all internships or new-grad roles, and none were submitted. Nineteen rows went through state/ledger_outbox.json until the other session copied them in. At the final sync the ledger showed 116 rows: 85 Ready to submit, 28 To apply, 2 Posting closed and 1 Rejected.
