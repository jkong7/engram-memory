# Applications and outreach from the 09-25/26 boards, then 7 more roles

Jonny asked for 15 roles from the recent boards to be filled in the browser, with outreach to recruiters from a fixed template. Chrome's Claude extension was disconnected until a full restart. About 8 applications were filled to the Submit step, and 15 outreach messages (8 LinkedIn InMails, 7 Gmail) went out before Jonny declined those roles and asked for them removed from the ledger and queue. Later that day, Quora, Fervo, Rockwell and iRhythm were filled to Submit, and Air Apps and Gallatin were filled; Zelos and IBM were blocked. Five outreach emails were written as unsent Gmail drafts, and a filled Quora form was accidentally navigated and then refilled.
