# Chipotle reorder blocked by payment

Jonathan asked whether Claude could place DoorDash and Chipotle orders for him. Neither has a connector, so the assistant said it would order through Claude in Chrome using his existing logged-in sessions and always confirm before placing an order. When Jonathan asked to reorder his last Chipotle order, the cart was built to checkout, but the order could not be submitted because the saved payment methods were expired or empty. Nothing was charged.
