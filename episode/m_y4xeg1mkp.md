# Overnight sweep repair and application batch

Jonny asked why the nightly job sweep kept failing. The cause was merge conflicts in state/queue.json during the push step and an unreliable GitHub scheduler, and both were fixed and verified with a clean run. Jonny then had Klaviyo, Perplexity, and a batch of new roles filled in Chrome and stopped before Submit, and he submitted about 19 of them on 2026-09-28. The ledger and blocklist were reconciled against his application list, and BlackRock and Valkyrie assessments and the Trulioo and Ramp rejections were recorded. Two earlier Chrome fills (Divergent and Instead) turned out to be repeats of existing applications and were flagged.
