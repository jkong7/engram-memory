# Sleeper Week 3 waiver claims: Gordon in, Lloyd cancelled

Jonathan asked for a review of his 2-0 Sleeper team, which was trailing iambip 54.7 to 63.1 in Week 3 after De'Von Achane was ruled out with a knee injury. The assistant recommended a $25 to $35 claim on Ollie Gordon (MIA RB) dropping Isaiah Likely, plus a backup claim on Alvin Kamara. The assistant submitted Gordon at $30 and Kamara at $10 (the bid amount was chosen by the assistant because Jonathan gave none), and it flagged a conflict where an earlier Lloyd claim also dropped Likely. Jonathan compared Gordon and MarShawn Lloyd, then had the Lloyd claim cancelled. Pending at the end: Gordon ($30, processes Wed 2:05 AM CDT) and Kamara ($10, processes Sun 11:05 PM CDT). A trade offer to stickyvickys (Irving for Meyers, Addison and Fannin) was still unanswered and could invalidate the Kamara claim.
