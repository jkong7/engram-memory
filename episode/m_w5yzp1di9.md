# Ledger sync, startup Door Plan and form filling

Jonny had the ledger synced to Gmail and marked Ramp and IMC as withdrawn, and logged Snowflake, Netic, Capital One and other applications as applied. A 149-startup Door Plan was built, the job-search queue was changed to rank big-name employers higher, and the pipeline was fixed to strip check marks from company names. Forms were filled in Chrome for about 25 applications and stopped before Submit, with outreach drafts saved in Gmail. Several browser tabs froze and some answers were lost and refilled.
