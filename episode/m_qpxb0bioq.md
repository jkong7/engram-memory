# Burner replies blocked by daily allowance

On 2026-10-10 Jonny's new guest life used up its $1.50 per-player daily AI allowance on background photos, which silently blocked replies from Maya and Danny. The assistant deployed a fix that exempts replies in a player's own conversations from that allowance, then re-queued the dropped replies. Jonny then asked why a feed post about Maya and Julian appeared alongside his matches, and the assistant traced it to a Seasons storyteller beat, noting a rendering bug where the photo's subject was wrong.
