# Lean Mac cleanup and iMessage routing for brain updates

Jonny asked for a leaner Mac, so Postgres and Redis were stopped and removed from login, about 25 GB of caches, worktrees and videos were cleared, and Cursor, Anki, the Claude VM and Granola's login item were removed. The `lean` scan tool was built in flow-kit and pushed as 0.6.0. Triage was rerouted through an Instinct project, and brain updates were moved to iMessage delivery through Instinct. Jonny also chose a free, read-only Fidelity Full View setup for finances over paid apps.
