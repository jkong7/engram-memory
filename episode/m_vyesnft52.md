# People-domain market gap research

Jonny asked for high-conviction people-domain product gaps built on primitive desires (status, attractiveness, FOMO, sex, relationships), backed by heavy research. The assistant ran about 40 web searches across dating, looksmaxxing, loneliness, AI companions, status signaling, fertility, prediction markets and related areas, then published a ten-idea dossier. The assistant published a 21-day scan build spec and a Tier product and go-to-market spec, with the thesis that inclusion beats the exclusive velvet-rope model.
