# Instagram Reels feed research and short-form video drafts (2026-09-25)

Jonny asked the agent to scroll his logged-in Instagram Reels feed, summarize each reel and its comments without censorship, and log the results as data for engagement research. The agent logged about 73 reels through web endpoints and then 16 through manual watching, but the hidden Chrome window blocked video playback and feed loading for much of the session. Research agents covered Instagram ranking and YouTube, post times and audio were pulled, and 48 of 49 audio files were transcribed locally. The session ended with a thesis and 10 short-form video drafts in ~/dev/reels-research/video-drafts.md, with an evidence ledger listing each rule's source and confidence.
