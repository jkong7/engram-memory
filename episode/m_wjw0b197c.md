# Apple Notes migration to Raycast and Obsidian vault

On 2026-10-04 Jonny asked to move his 63 Apple Notes into Raycast with the same folders. Keystroke automation damaged one of his existing Raycast notes, which he said he did not mind. The agent instead wrote the notes as Markdown into a separate Notes vault in the Obsidian iCloud folder and backed it up to jkong7/notes. Jonny then moved the brain vault into iCloud and granted zsh Full Disk Access. He asked to keep everything inside Raycast, so the agent built the My Notes extension, and he removed the Obsidian extension.
