# Open roles at untouched companies

Jonny pasted a long list of target tech companies and asked which have open new-grad, intern or early-career software roles he had not applied to. The assistant matched the list against his 710-row application ledger, found about 75 companies already applied to or blocked by the one-application-per-company rule, and checked the careers sites of the rest using parallel research agents. The results went into a private artifact with 8 strong picks, 15 roles with catches, and the remaining companies grouped by status. Some agents stalled and some careers sites blocked checks, so those companies remain unverified.
