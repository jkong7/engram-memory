# YouTube virality research and faceless video production

Jonathan asked for transcripts from other creators' videos on venting and doom topics. The assistant summarized them rather than republishing full transcripts, then ran a larger virality study across about 560 videos and genres. The research produced a thesis, posting-time and thumbnail findings, and three video plans. The assistant built five unposted faceless videos (F1-F5) in ~/dev/doom-videos. Jonathan asked for concise status answers and for the F4 and F5 topics on turning 25 and looks.
