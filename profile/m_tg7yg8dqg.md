# Jonathan has LinkedIn Premium and wants LinkedIn InMail plus email as primary cold-outreach channels

Jonathan has LinkedIn Premium and wants to use it. He considers LinkedIn (InMail) plus
email the best way into companies that have no early-career posting, which is most of the
AI-scribe competitor list he compiled.

**Why:** Premium gives InMail credits and lets him message people he is not connected to,
so outreach plans should lead with a named contact and an InMail, not only a careers-page
application.

**How to apply:** When a target company has no intern or new-grad req, build the plan
around a specific person (engineer or hiring manager first, recruiter second) with an
InMail draft plus an email fallback, rather than telling him to wait for a posting.
See [[jobsearch-pipeline]] and [[ai-scribe-target-list]].
