# Filling Workday application forms

Workday gotchas: the resume parse creates duplicate or mis-titled work and education entries, so check each entry and delete duplicates with that entry's own delete button. Save and Continue only saves the step and moves to the next page. Opening the degree dropdown froze BMO's page and blocked screenshots, so pick the degree with one click and type-ahead. Long yes/no labels often fail with typing plus Enter, so click the option. If the Chrome tab group disappears mid-fill, reopen the posting in the group before continuing.
