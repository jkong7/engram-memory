# Open the Verdict app locally to look at it

Run `cd ~/dev/verdict && swift run verdict-mac --demo` to open the app as a phone-sized Mac window preloaded with a 16-bottle demo shelf. The first screen is AI consent, and the tabs after it are Today, Buy? and Shelf. The window is untested beyond the consent screen unless it has been clicked through.
