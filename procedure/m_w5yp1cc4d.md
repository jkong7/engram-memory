# Filling custom dropdowns on application forms in Chrome

Synthetic clicks and events often do not open custom dropdown menus on application forms. The reliable method is to click the real field, type to filter the options, then select with a real key press. Keep the form tab in the foreground while it fills, because background tabs can freeze or reload and lose answers. Re-check forms after a reload.
