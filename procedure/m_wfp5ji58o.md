# Filling job application forms in Chrome background tabs

Keep the application tab in front while filling it. In a hidden tab, scripted dropdown selections stall, coordinate clicks miss, and school or year pickers stop rendering. Use element-reference clicks instead, or JS option clicks for EEO dropdowns. Workday sign-in and Create Account steps need Jonny to do them himself.
