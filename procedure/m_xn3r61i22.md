# Fixing Workday resume autofill work history

Workday's resume autofill can split one job into two broken entries and put the company name into the title field, as happened with Abridge and WristBuddy. Fix it by keeping the entry that has the title and description, adding the company and dates to it, then deleting the broken duplicate. Check every work-history entry after autofill.
