# Content research should not be softened or censored

Jonny wants content-research outputs to include the raw material, including slurs, sexual content and mean comments, quoted accurately without moralizing or softening. He uses this data to understand what generates clicks and views. Use judgment about the research goal, but do not hold back on the content itself.
