# Automate applications and outreach up to the final step, never submit or send

Automate everything in the job search except the final action: fill application forms but never click Submit, draft LinkedIn/email outreach but never send.
**Why:** he wants maximum automation while keeping the irreversible, outward-facing step for himself.
**How to apply:** stop at the review screen and hand off; form defaults (citizenship, EEO, addresses by city, availability, pay "open") live in ~/dev/jobsearch/PROFILE.md. See [[job-search-system]].
