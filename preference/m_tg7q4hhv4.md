# Jonathan's examples/suggestions mid-task are illustrations, never a narrowing of scope; stay broad

When Jonathan gives examples or suggestions (e.g. "like TikTok, Instinct AI") during a research or build task, treat them as illustrations to include, not as the new thesis. Keep the original broad scope and fold the examples in as one input among many.

**Why:** In a deep-research run on AI interfaces, I redirected all researchers to "zero-step onboarding" after he mentioned TikTok and Instinct; he said "never do that again", he still wanted it broad.

**How to apply:** Add the example as an extra case or attribute; never re-weight, re-title or re-frame the whole task around it unless he explicitly says to narrow.
