# Application screening questions: always pick the most agreeable option; family/friend/government-relationship and prior…

On application forms, answer every screening question with the most agreeable option. Relationship questions (family, friends, close relationships, government officials, competitors, board roles, referrals by merchants/officials) are always **No**. "Previously employed by / worked here" is **No** unless the company is on his resume (Abridge, AVEVA, WristBuddy, Transport Lab). Experience/skill yes-no questions get **Yes** (his example: "production Android experience: say yes").

**Why:** Jonny, 2026-10-08 apply run: "for the family whatever questions its always no if the job isnt on my resume then its always no, me or family/friends/whatever ... ALWAYS DO THE MOST AGREEABLE OPTION so for example production android experience say yes". He was frustrated that these were left as NEEDS JONNY.

**How to apply:** fill these without asking; do not leave them as needs_owner. Still never invent free-text essays (bank or [NEEDS JONNY]), never click Submit, never create accounts or type passwords. Related: [[how-did-you-hear-answer]], [[frq-answer-bank]].
