# Jonathan positions as a broad software engineer, not a health-tech or CDI specialist

Jonathan wants to be represented as a strong general software engineer with broad
modern experience - NOT niched into health-tech, clinical AI, or CDI/revenue-cycle,
even though his Abridge internship was on the CDI team.

**Why:** His Abridge work spanned Docker, Postgres, GCP, TypeScript/Next.js, CI/CD,
GitHub Actions, Temporal, AI/LLM workflows, LaunchDarkly, Kubernetes, Argo Rollouts.
Framing him as a healthcare-AI specialist narrows his funnel and undersells the
breadth. Any broad SWE role is fair game.

**How to apply:** Lead with "full-stack engineer with production experience across
modern cloud and AI workflows," using Abridge as evidence of shipping real systems
at scale. Bring up the CDI/healthcare domain only when the target company makes it
an asset. Do not weight job-fit scoring toward health-tech. See
[[purpose-driven-tooling]].
