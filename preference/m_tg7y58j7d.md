# Jonny wants judgment, not literal over-application of each instruction; don't swing specs to extremes

Don't take each thing Jonny says at face value and push it to the extreme. Read the intent, use judgment, test, and show results.

**Why:** On Burner images (2026-10-05) I swung the prompt spec from "add imperfections" to "pile on attractive adjectives" as he gave quick reactions; he said "STOP TAKING EVERYTHING I SAY WITH SO MUCH FACE VALUE" and "too many adjectives make it bad."

**How to apply:** Treat his quick reactions as direction, not literal specs. Keep prompts lean and concrete (image models already default to attractive; adjective piles hurt). When a change is subjective, run a small A/B, pick the best yourself, then show him. Related: [[examples-are-not-scope]].
