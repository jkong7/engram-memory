# Finish evidence gathering before drafting deliverables

For research-to-deliverable tasks, Jonny wants every evidence gap closed before drafting final outputs, and he does not want slow or rushed drafts. He asks for a complete evidence base, with sources and confidence levels, first. Drafts come after that. Background work should not interrupt the main line of work.
