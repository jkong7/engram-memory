# Additional voice avoid patterns

Jonny's writing must also avoid: rhetorical questions answered right after they're asked, "Then"/"And then" before every sudden moment, chopped near-synonym triples for drama, one sentence per line for every beat, cold named emotions instead of felt ones, repeated sentence structure and crutch words, and fake corporate friendliness.
