# Jonny's top priority for the brain system is how easily he sees updates; results must show up in the pushes he already…

"The most important thing is ease of which I see updates" (2026-10-04). Background work that only lands in Todoist moves, vault files or `.state` logs is effectively invisible to him.

**Why:** he asked where Capture Triage shows up and the honest answer was "mostly nowhere"; he relies on the Claude push notifications (1:30 Brief, 6pm Career Sync, 9pm Evening Digest).

**How to apply:** any new routine or agent feature must say how its result reaches him, preferably as a line in an existing push rather than a new notification. Things that need his input carry the Todoist label `needs-you`, which shows in the pinned "Needs me" filter (@needs-you, the 3rd and last filter on the free plan). On 2026-10-04 the Brief got "Needs you" + "Since last brief" sections and push lines, and the Evening Digest got "Filed:" and "Needs you:" lines.
