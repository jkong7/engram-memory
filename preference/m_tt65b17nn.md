# No questions during long autonomous build goals

Jonny wants the agent to keep going end to end on long autonomous build goals without pausing to ask questions. Unresolved questions should be decided with judgment and reported in the final summary. Why: during the chartside build (2026-09-27) he told the agent to swallow questions and not stop until the goal was reached.
