# Before writing anything sent/shared/posted as Jonny (emails, apps, messages, docs, drafts), read ~/brain/me/voice.md an…

Read `~/brain/me/voice.md` before writing anything sent, shared, posted, forwarded or presented as Jonny's writing, including drafts he might send himself. It is the canonical rule set (v6 voice reference, facts-only, avoid list, the four once-per-application extras, final check, answer cache statuses). The jobsearch `WRITING_RULES.md` and `writing/V6_STYLE.md` follow it.

**Why:** Jonny set these as standing rules on 2026-10-04 and wanted them in his brain vault, not in global CLAUDE.md ("claude md is too global").

**How to apply:** Open voice.md first, then v6 files for voice. When he corrects or edits wording, update voice.md (and the jobsearch files if it is about applications). Rules never grant permission to send. Related: [[no-em-dashes]], [[no-sending-messages]], [[frq-answer-bank]], [[goes-by-jonny]].
