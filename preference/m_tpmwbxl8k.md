# Primitive-desire product ideas, research first

Jonathan wants people-domain product ideas built on primitive desires (status, attractiveness, FOMO, rivalry, sex, relationships), framed boldly without hedging. He ranks research depth above building: he wants many high-quality ideas, each heavily sourced with reasons it can succeed.
