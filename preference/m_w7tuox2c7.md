# Answer for \"How did you hear about us\" on applications: always pick Referral/Referred/Employee Referral whenever it i…

On application forms, "How did you hear about us / Source": **always pick the referral option** ("Referral", "Referred", "Employee Referral", or similar) whenever one exists, even if it opens a follow-up. Only when no referral option exists, pick a campus option ("Campus", "University", "Career fair" style), and only then a website/job board.

**Why:** Jonathan said on 2026-09-28: "always pick referred/referall if given a chance". This supersedes his earlier rule that skipped Referral when the form asked for a referrer's name.

**Exception (2026-09-30):** when the referral option requires a referrer name/email or states an employee actively engaged him (e.g. LiveRamp Workday "I was referred (actively engaged to apply to this role) by a LiveRamp Employee") and he has no real referrer there, he chose "switch to university/college/fair" instead. Use the campus/university option (e.g. "University Event", "University Applicants").

**How to apply:** every form fill, including Workday source pickers (open the Employee Referral branch) and [[jobsearch-pipeline]] routine packets. If a referral follow-up asks for a referrer name or email, leave that sub-field for Jonathan rather than inventing one.
