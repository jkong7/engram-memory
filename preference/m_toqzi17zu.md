# Outreach messages lead with the Abridge hook and cite specific work after research

Jonny wants company outreach (LinkedIn and email) to lead with his Abridge CDI internship as the hook for AI-scribe companies, saying he loved the work and was passionate about it. Messages should name concrete work (SOAP notes, A&P, billing codes, coding alignment with coders) and say how he would contribute. He wants people researched before drafting, and a claim about someone's role must be verified before it goes in a message. He asked for longer, specific messages over short generic ones.
