# Footwear variety list for Burner photos

For Burner footwear photos, Jonny wants variety from this list: strappy sandals, heeled sandals, open-toed heels, slingback open-toed heels, wedges, slides, platform sandals, flip-flops, dress sandals, mid-removal, and barefoot where it fits. Footwear should be styled into street-style outfit photos, not framed around feet.
