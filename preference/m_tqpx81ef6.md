# Chicago outreach email template

Jonny's Chicago outreach emails use one fixed template: no company-specific line in paragraph 1, and one company-specific sentence at the end of paragraph 2 starting 'On that note, I would love to contribute to what [Company] is building.' That closing detail is short and plain. Only primarily software roles based in Chicago qualify.
