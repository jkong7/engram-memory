# Job application ledger artifact and total counter

Jonny's job application ledger is a Claude artifact at https://claude.ai/artifact/9c6SB1fRo6xMSpaNrVFa6V. Its total-applied counter counts sent applications and sent outreach, including rows later moved to OA, Interview or Rejected. Drafts and To apply rows are excluded.
