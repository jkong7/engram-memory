# Burner support address

burnerapp.help@gmail.com is Burner's public support address, shown on the Safety, Terms, Privacy and report pages. Sign-in emails are sent from it through Gmail SMTP, up to 30 an hour. Mail sent to it lands in that Gmail account, not Jonny's main inbox.
