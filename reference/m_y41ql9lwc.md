# loom publisher and repo

~/dev/loom is being published to the private GitHub repo jkong7/loom (set up 2026-10-08). A launchd agent, com.jkong7.loom-publish, pushes its queued commits one at a time with random 10 to 90 minute gaps. Its log is ~/Library/Logs/loom-publish.log.
